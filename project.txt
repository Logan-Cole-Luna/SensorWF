The Satellite Learning Laboratory (SATLL) is a modular training platform
designed to replicate the core subsystems of a small satellite - including power,
communications, attitude control, and thermal systems. This project utilizes AI-
driven anomaly detection techniques to analyze SATLL telemetry streams in real-
time, identifying abnormal behavior in subsystems. Early anomaly detection can
prevent mission failure, enhance spacecraft safety, and support autonomous
operations.
Data Collection
Telemetry streams (Table 1) are logged at 1 Hz from power attitude, thermal, and
communication subsystems,. Data is exported in structured formats (CSV/HDF5).
Power: battery voltage, solar array output
Attitude: gyro rates, reaction wheel speed, magnetometer vectors
Thermal: board and panel temperature sensors
Comms: signal strength, packet success rate, latency
Anomaly Injection
Simulated fault conditions include voltage drops, comms degradation, and reaction
wheel saturation to mimic common on-orbit issues.
AI Techniques
Baseline: z-score thresholding
Advanced: Isolation Forest and autoencoders for multivariate anomaly detection
Performance is evaluated by detection accuracy, false positive rate, and detection
